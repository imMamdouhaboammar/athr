# AthR Domain Context

AthR is a professional work network for digital marketing specialists where discovery and hiring are grounded in work evidence, professional context, and confirmed work relationships

## Language

**Specialist**:
A person who offers digital marketing expertise and can publish professional work, become discoverable, collaborate, and receive opportunities
_Avoid_: Freelancer when referring to the person's permanent identity, talent

**Hiring User**:
A person using AthR to find, evaluate, contact, or hire specialists, whether independently or on behalf of a company
_Avoid_: Client when no commercial relationship exists yet, employer

**Professional Intent**:
What a user currently wants to do on AthR, such as find work, hire, or both; it is not a permanent account type
_Avoid_: User type, role when referring to account identity

**Work Evidence**:
Published material that demonstrates a specialist's relevant professional contribution, context, and outcomes
_Avoid_: Content when the primary purpose is proving professional work

**Portfolio Project**:
A structured record of professional work owned by a specialist and prepared for publication as evidence
_Avoid_: Post, gallery item

**Case Study**:
The public presentation of a published Portfolio Project, including problem context, personal contribution, work, collaborators, and outcomes where disclosure is permitted
_Avoid_: Portfolio card

**Work Metric**:
A result attached to a Portfolio Project, with a visibility and verification state
_Avoid_: KPI when referring to the stored evidence object itself

**Verification State**:
The status describing how a work claim or relationship has been substantiated, such as self-reported or client-verified
_Avoid_: Rating

**Collaborator**:
A Specialist identified as having contributed to the same Portfolio Project, whose relationship may be confirmed independently
_Avoid_: Teammate when the relationship has not been established

**Work Relationship**:
A confirmed professional connection supported by project, client, collaborator, or other accepted work evidence
_Avoid_: Connection when referring specifically to verified professional history

**Recommendation**:
A professional endorsement from one user to another that can be attached to a skill, project, or work relationship
_Avoid_: Review when it is not a transaction rating

**Opportunity**:
A published request for specialist work with enough context for a specialist to assess relevance and respond
_Avoid_: Job when the engagement may be freelance, retainer, consulting, or project-based

**Project Brief**:
The structured description of a hiring need before or as it becomes an Opportunity
_Avoid_: Job description

**Application**:
A Specialist's explicit response to an Opportunity, optionally supported by selected Work Evidence and a proposal note
_Avoid_: Bid when price competition is not the defining behavior

**Invite**:
A Hiring User's request for a specific Specialist to consider an Opportunity
_Avoid_: Application

**Service**:
A repeatable professional offering a Specialist chooses to publish with a defined outcome and optional commercial framing
_Avoid_: Gig

**Availability**:
A Specialist's current capacity and preferred engagement modes for new work
_Avoid_: Online status

**Work Feed**:
A professional feed centered on Work Evidence, useful breakdowns, experiments, collaboration requests, availability, and Opportunities
_Avoid_: Social feed

**Qualified Work Conversation**:
A conversation started with clear professional context and plausible fit between a hiring need and a Specialist
_Avoid_: Message when measuring product value

**Crew**:
A future reusable group of Specialists who intentionally present themselves as a working unit for suitable engagements
_Avoid_: Agency unless it is legally or operationally one

**Specialty**:
A broad digital marketing discipline used to describe a Specialist's primary professional area, such as Paid Social or SEO
_Avoid_: Skill when the concept is the broader discipline

**Skill**:
A narrower capability within or across Specialties used for discovery, evidence, and recommendations
_Avoid_: Specialty

**Market**:
A geographic market in which work experience or an Opportunity is relevant
_Avoid_: Location when referring to professional market experience

**Company**:
An organization represented on AthR by one or more users for hiring or professional identity purposes
_Avoid_: Client unless an actual work relationship exists
