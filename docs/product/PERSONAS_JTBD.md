# AthR Personas and Jobs to Be Done

Personas in AthR describe recurring professional situations, not permanent account types

A single user can move between several situations over time

## 1. Independent Specialist

### Situation

A digital marketing professional needs a credible professional presence and a steady path to relevant work without relying only on referrals, generic job boards, or social reach

### Core Jobs

- When I meet a potential client, help me show the work most relevant to their need so I can establish credibility quickly
- When I become available, help relevant buyers discover that availability without requiring constant self-promotion
- When I apply for work, help me attach evidence that makes the fit obvious instead of writing the same generic pitch repeatedly
- When I collaborated on strong work, help me represent my contribution accurately without claiming the whole project
- When my work has confidential details, help me publish useful evidence without exposing information I should protect

### Success Signals

- First credible Case Study published
- Relevant profile visits from Work Evidence
- Qualified Work Conversations
- Invites and Applications tied to relevant evidence
- Confirmed Collaborators and Recommendations

### Main Risks

- Empty-profile abandonment
- Pressure to exaggerate results
- Popularity signals replacing professional relevance
- Too much effort required to maintain the profile

## 2. Senior Specialist With Strong Referral Network

### Situation

An experienced marketer already gets work through reputation but needs a better public professional record, selective inbound demand, and a way to make previous collaboration visible

### Core Jobs

- When someone asks what I have done in a specific category, help me send one relevant professional page instead of assembling proof manually
- When I only want a small amount of new work, help me signal limited Availability without opening a flood of generic requests
- When I trust other specialists, help my Work Relationships make future collaboration and referrals easier
- When I receive an Opportunity, help me evaluate its quality before spending time replying

### Success Signals

- High-quality inbound over high-volume inbound
- Saved profiles and Case Studies from relevant Hiring Users
- Repeat Collaborator signals
- Invitations with clear professional context

## 3. Emerging Specialist

### Situation

A capable marketer has limited brand recognition and may have fewer client names or large public metrics but still needs a fair way to demonstrate ability

### Core Jobs

- When I lack a famous client list, help the quality and relevance of my work carry more weight than my audience size
- When some results cannot be verified, help me distinguish honest self-reported evidence from unsupported claims
- When I build new experience, help each completed project strengthen future discovery

### Success Signals

- Discovery through Skills and relevant Work Evidence
- Portfolio completion without requiring famous brands
- Confirmed collaboration and Recommendation growth

## 4. Founder or Small Business Hiring User

### Situation

A non-specialist knows the business outcome they need but may not know the exact marketing title, channel mix, or terminology required

### Core Jobs

- When I know the problem but not the job title, help me describe the need in business and marketing context
- When I evaluate people, help me see evidence that resembles my situation before I start a conversation
- When I contact someone, help us begin with the same project context so I do not repeat the brief
- When I receive Applications, help me compare fit without turning the process into price-only bidding

### Success Signals

- Project Brief completed
- Relevant Case Studies viewed
- Shortlist built
- Qualified Work Conversation started
- Clear next action after the conversation

## 5. Marketing Lead or CMO

### Situation

An experienced marketing leader needs specialized external capacity, often for a particular channel, Market, project phase, or temporary gap

### Core Jobs

- When I need specialist depth, help me filter by relevant professional context rather than generic seniority
- When a candidate claims results, help me understand the evidence and Verification State
- When I have a trusted Specialist, help me discover confirmed Collaborators who may fit adjacent work
- When hiring is sensitive, keep drafts, saves, Applications, and conversations private

### Success Signals

- Faster relevant shortlist creation
- Evidence-based evaluation
- Invite acceptance
- Repeat use for future specialist needs

## 6. Agency Lead

### Situation

An agency needs flexible specialist capacity for client work and may repeatedly hire people across channels, Markets, and project types

### Core Jobs

- When capacity changes, help me find Specialists who are available for the right engagement model
- When client confidentiality matters, help me evaluate work without forcing public disclosure
- When I find reliable people, help confirmed Work Relationships reduce future search effort
- When several complementary Specialists work well together, preserve that context for future Crews without pretending they are an agency today

### Success Signals

- Repeat Specialist hiring
- Repeat Collaborator relationships
- Saved private shortlists
- Future Crew formation when the product supports it

## 7. Specialist Acting as a Hiring User

### Situation

A Specialist wins a project and needs another professional to complete part of the work

### Core Jobs

- When I need complementary expertise, let me hire from the same identity I use to publish work
- When I contact another Specialist, let the conversation reference the actual project need
- When the collaboration is completed, help both sides confirm the Work Relationship accurately

### Success Signals

- No second account required
- Collaboration request converts to conversation
- Confirmed shared Work Evidence

## Shared Job Principles

Across all personas, AthR should prioritize:

1. Relevant proof before popularity
2. Professional context before generic messaging
3. Current intent before permanent account labels
4. Confirmed work relationships before vanity connection counts
5. Honest evidence states before false certainty
